What I Learned

This model served as an exploration into standard convolutional feature extraction. I learned how spatial pooling reduces dimensionality while maintaining important features like edges and curves. Exporting the Keras model to tfjs_model and running inference client-side demonstrated the capability of edge-AI, ensuring low latency and privacy since no image data leaves the browser.
